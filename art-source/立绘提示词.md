# 立绘提示词（Gemini）

## 怎么用

- 每次把三视图（`立绘/三视图.jpg`）作为参考图一起上传。
- 先粘「通用前缀」，再接一条动作描述。
- 一张图只画一个动作。

## 通用前缀

```
Use the attached character sheet as the exact reference. Same character: chibi girl with long wavy dark-blue hair fading to light blue at the tips, an ahoge, an orange star hair clip, navy whale-fin ears, a navy whale tail, cream hoodie with an orange bow, a "Claude" name badge on a lanyard, orange flower patches, tan pleated skirt with a white frill, cream leg warmers with orange flowers, tan shoes. Keep exactly the same art style, colors, line weight and proportions (about 2.5 heads tall). Full body, feet fully visible, nothing cropped. Solid flat pure green background (#00FF00), no gradient, no floor, no shadow, no effect symbols, no extra text, no watermark. High resolution.
```

## 小提醒

- **用纯绿背景：** 她的卫衣和袜子是奶白色，白底抠图容易把衣服也抠掉。
- **不要删「no effect symbols」：** 爱心、Zzz、汗滴这些由程序画，图里干净一点更好抠。
- **检查细节：** 脸或衣服走样，或者工牌、星星发夹、鲸鱼耳朵、尾巴没了，就重新生成。
- **眨眼、笑眼和尾巴摆动不用另画：** 这几个是程序在三视图上改出来的。
